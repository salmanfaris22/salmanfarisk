About

I'm a motion designer who also writes the code for the page.

In After Effects I work on titles, logo reveals and kinetic type, where a few frames of timing decide whether a move feels sharp or sloppy. In HTML and CSS I turn designs into fast, readable pages that work on a phone as well as a desktop.

I like projects where motion has a job: guiding the eye, confirming a click, or giving a brand a pulse.
